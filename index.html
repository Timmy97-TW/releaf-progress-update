Beyond that we have one comparison point against BioDrop, with no linearity curve, no limit of detection and no repeatability figure. That is the largest gap here; a new calibration run began on 15 August.

    膜不會先壞。剪切率、通量、臨界通量餘裕、跨膜壓力在 1 倍、10 倍、100 倍下完全相同， van Reis 的線性放大在這裡站得住。先出問題的是另外兩個：供電在 10 倍時就需要三倍以上的板子， 控制迴路的 θ/τ 在 10 倍到 100 倍之間越過 1，也就是 CcaSR 的響應時間開始長過系統的滯留時間。

    The membrane is not what breaks. Shear, flux, critical-flux margin and TMP are identical at 1×, 10× and 100×, so van Reis linear scaling holds. Two other things go first. Power needs more than triple the panel area by 10×. And the control loop crosses θ/τ = 1 somewhere between 10× and 100×, where the CcaSR response time grows longer than the system residence time.

    附帶說明：θ 取 CcaSR 的 t½ON ≈ 105 分鐘加上每個模組 3 分鐘的管路延遲；TMP 是依通量估的示意值， 不是實測；qO₂ 與 C_w/C_b 是文獻典型值，不是菌株專屬。這些都要用實測換掉。 Caveats: θ takes the CcaSR t½ON of about 105 minutes plus three minutes of line delay per module; TMP is estimated from flux, not measured; qO₂ and C_w/C_b are literature-typical rather than strain-specific. All of these want replacing with measurements.
