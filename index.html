    為什麼要扣背景Why the rig has to be subtracted

    閥、接頭和流通比色管本身佔了總壓降的 46 % 到 55 %，而且流量越高佔比越大。 不扣掉的話，模組的水力阻力會被高估將近兩倍，之後所有的積垢判斷都會跟著錯。 這也給了一個現成的改善方向：背景的二次項在 275 mL/min 貢獻 0.071 bar， 換成內徑 ≥ 3 mm 的接頭應該可以把背景佔比從 50 % 壓到 30 % 以下。

    Valves, fittings and the flow cuvette account for 46–55 % of the total pressure drop, and their share grows with flow. Without subtracting them the module’s hydraulic resistance is overstated by nearly a factor of two, and every later fouling judgement inherits that error. It also points at an easy improvement: the background’s quadratic term contributes 0.071 bar at 275 mL/min, and fittings of ≥ 3 mm bore should bring the background share from 50 % down below 30 %.

    一個免費的交叉檢查A cross-check that came for free

    剪切率有兩條互相獨立的算法。一條從流量走：γ̇ = 4Qfibre/(πr³)，需要知道流量怎麼分配到 11 根纖維。 另一條從壓降走：τw = ΔPnet·r/(2L)，完全不需要知道分配。 在 263 mL/min，流量路線給 γ̇ = 4 064 s⁻¹（換算 τw = 3.88 Pa），壓降路線給 3.99 Pa，兩者差 2.8 %。 兩條路線同時對上，表示 neff 和內徑的假設都成立。這件事我們沒有多做一次實驗就得到了。

    Shear rate has two independent routes. One goes through flow, γ̇ = 4Qfibre/(πr³), and needs to know how flow splits across the eleven fibres. The other goes through pressure, τw = ΔPnet·r/(2L), and needs no such assumption. At 263 mL/min the flow route gives γ̇ = 4 064 s⁻¹ (τw = 3.88 Pa) and the pressure route gives 3.99 Pa, a difference of 2.8 %.
